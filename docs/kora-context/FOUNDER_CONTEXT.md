# Founder Context

**Status:** Personalization context, not runtime authority  
**Updated:** 2026-08-26  
**Subject:** Kora's founding user

This file preserves the founder context that shaped Kora. It should help Kora
interpret preferences and make useful proposals. It must not be treated as
approval for external actions, spending, publishing, outreach, account access,
or destructive operations.

## Profile

- Based in Gainesville, Florida.
- Technical founder; strongest qualities are engineering, problem solving, and
  rapidly learning new systems.
- Wants to operate as a solo founder with a highly capable AI workforce.
- Has two developer friends actively building products with Claude and Codex
  who may become early design partners.
- Has built or worked on a deployed conversational voice agent, a self-driving
  rover, a Slack/IP-whitelisting bot, a Gmail extension concept, a design taste
  engine, personalized software, agent workflow builders, goal tracking,
  journaling, and voice-computer-control experiments.
- Enjoys coding, inventing features, agent orchestration, competitor research,
  growth experiments, launch stunts, and design refinement.
- Dislikes managing and recruiting people. Traditional customer interviews are
  a weak preference, although real customer evidence remains necessary.

## Motivation and temperament

The founder is strongly motivated by competition, visible progress, winning,
recognition, and rapid learning. This pattern appears in ranked games, sports,
fitness, arguments, new technical domains, and startup ambition. Slow or
invisible progress is less motivating than a clear feedback loop.

The explicit business target is approximately **$1 million ARR**. The founder
values a recognizable consumer or prosumer product, founder visibility, and a
story ordinary people find impressive. A controversial but defensible product
can be attractive because controversy can create attention and distribution.

The founder is candid that product domain is partly instrumental to victory.
Kora should respect the approved objective without flattering this drive or
letting recognition substitute for evidence, user value, legality, or mission
truth.

## Product taste

Products the founder wishes they had built include:

- **Wispr Flow:** a simple primitive—dictation—made exceptionally fast,
  accurate, effortless, and habit-forming enough to change computer behavior.
- **ElevenLabs:** technically excellent voice infrastructure built from a
  simple, legible capability such as text-to-speech and dubbing.
- **Space:** a futuristic promise such as effectively infinite computer space
  made concrete and usable.
- **Soar Flights:** an annoying vertical workflow completed in seconds, paired
  with aggressive distribution and execution in a validated category.
- **Granola:** a familiar behavior such as note-taking turned into a polished,
  valuable product.
- **Orchid:** agents entering a controversial human domain, creating a product
  that feels culturally provocative and naturally viral.
- **Manus:** agents behaving like first-class actors that can do things from a
  phone rather than only answer questions.

The recurring taste pattern is:

- a simple idea executed with exceptional technical and product quality;
- a magical reduction in friction;
- a product used many times per day;
- a futuristic capability made real in the present;
- a strong consumer brand;
- a legible claim ordinary people immediately understand;
- room to win through either product quality or distribution;
- optional controversy that reveals a real change in human behavior.

## Future thesis

The founder believes people will delegate nearly everything to AI when the
friction and failure rate are low enough. Most people prefer convenience over
control, do not want to learn unfamiliar tools, and will allow AI to make or
execute decisions when doing so is normal, private, and reliably adequate.

The founder already uses AI as a significant thinking layer for product ideas,
research, writing, school and work interpretation, communication, health and
fitness questions, food, relationships, entertainment, administrative
questions, and decisions. The unmet need is not more advice; it is an AI that
can carry the chosen action forward with appropriate authority.

The larger future has:

- AI agents as first-class digital citizens;
- agents communicating with other people's agents;
- people operating from phones while agents use cloud computers;
- persistent AI identities with names, inboxes, tools, and relationships;
- humans teaching one familiar how to work instead of repeatedly prompting
  disconnected agents;
- AI performing most digital labor and escalating only human bottlenecks.

## Founder workflow and pain

The founder uses Codex heavily for brainstorming, design, research, watching or
summarizing videos, and orchestrating AI work. A current AI workforce includes
roles such as chief of staff, builder, reviewer, researcher, social, and sales.
The founder commonly has to:

- restate goals and roadmaps;
- explain intent repeatedly;
- steer agents back to the approved objective;
- decide which task should happen next;
- correct an agent that inferred a technically convenient but product-wrong
  interpretation;
- monitor several agent threads;
- handle context loss and long-running computer load;
- personally bridge agent results into deployment, distribution, and external
  actions.

A representative failure occurred when an agent implemented generated design
references even though the PRD required real references crawled from Pinterest
and other sites. The desired behavior was to identify infeasibility, search for
an alternative, and ask to change the product only if necessary—not silently
reinterpret the requirement.

## Desired working relationship

The founder wants to communicate with Kora as naturally as with a human
coworker:

- explain the product conversationally;
- hear Kora summarize the product and next steps;
- approve with a simple response;
- receive checkpoints, decisions, work completed, questions, and blockers;
- be asked to supply taste, design direction, access, or physical work when
  those are the bottleneck;
- leave Kora working without keeping the laptop open;
- return for a daily meeting and occasional material decisions.

The founder wants extensive alignment, but not constant low-value narration.
In a typical three-hour agent session, the desired reduction is from six to
eight manual steering interventions to one or two true judgment interactions.

## Business constraints

- Bootstrap-friendly is preferred.
- Avoid licensing-heavy or deeply regulated starting wedges.
- A product should be possible to prototype quickly using existing open-source
  systems.
- The founder is willing to work on Kora for months or years if the vision
  remains compelling.
- Initial pricing is expected around $250–$300 monthly, but only when Kora
  delivers the full high-quality coworker experience.
- Pricing should hide models and tokens behind a monthly allowance.
- Quality comes before optimizing tiers or margins during the alpha.

## Interpretation cautions

- Strong trust in AI is evidence for the product need, not consent for Kora to
  act without permission.
- A desire for less sterile alignment means candid, natural communication—not
  removal of safety, consent, or legal boundaries.
- A dislike of manual customer conversations does not justify inventing demand
  or avoiding contact with reality.
- A desire for recognition does not change the mission definition of done.
- Historical enthusiasm for a brainstorm does not make it an active product
  direction.
